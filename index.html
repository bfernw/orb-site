Orb

A dose that steps up when it's light and down when it's heavy.

Questions

What do Light and Heavy mean?
Light means the dose is getting easy. Heavy means it is a reach. You type the next number. Orb never changes it for you.

I missed a day.
That's fine. A missed day is only a missed day. If life gets in the way, pause the habit and today has nothing to tap.

How do I move to a new iPhone?
Turn on Sync with iCloud in settings on both phones, or tap Save a copy on the old phone and Bring back a copy on the new one.

The widget looks out of date.
Open Orb once. The widget updates when Orb does.

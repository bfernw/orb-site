Privacy

Orb collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

Where your habits live

Your habits and the days you mark are stored on your iPhone. The widgets read a small copy of them on the same phone.

iCloud

If you turn on Sync with iCloud, Apple keeps a copy in your own private iCloud so your other iPhones see the same habits. That copy is tied to your Apple Account. We cannot see it. Turn it off in Orb's settings at any time.

Copies you save

Save a copy writes a file to the place you choose in Files. Where it goes from there is up to you.

Feedback

If you email us, we see what you write and your email address. We use it only to reply.

Children

Orb collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so first, with the date.
